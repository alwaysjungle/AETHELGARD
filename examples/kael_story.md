# THE BLADE OF ETERNITY

**Genre:** Dark Fantasy
**Format:** Vertical Scroll (Webtoon)

## Premise
A world where every lie you tell becomes a physical scar on your body. The protagonist, Kael, is a "Truth-Seeker" who can see the weight of these scars.

## Character: Kael
- **Motivation:** To find the origin of the "Scar Curse" and cure it.
- **Flaw:** He is brutally honest, often hurting those he loves.
- **The Living Wound:** Every time he heals a scar, he loses a memory associated with the person he healed.

## Conflict
Truth (Kael) vs. Comfort (The High Priestess). The Priestess believes scars are necessary for social order.

## Factions
* The Truth-Seekers: Nomadic healers who can see and heal scars.
* The Order of Silence: The state religion that enforces the Scar Curse.

## Chapter 1 Outline
1. Kael arrives at a village where everyone is covered in scars.
2. He meets a young girl who is dying from an infected "Great Lie" scar.
3. Kael heals her, but forgets his own sister's name in the process.
4. The High Priestess's guards arrive to arrest him for "Truth-Heresy".
5. Kael must decide: surrender or use his power to reveal the guards' own lies.
